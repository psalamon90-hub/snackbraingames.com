Privacy policy

Arrowtopia: Arrow Puzzle and snackbraingames.com. Last updated: 25 November 2026.

Polska wersja

In short

  • The game has no accounts and no sign-in. We never ask for your name or email in the game.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings.
  • We do not sell personal data and we do not use analytics or tracking tools of our own.

1. Who we are

Snackbrain Games is the name under which Paweł Salamon publishes mobile games as part of his business Paweł Salamon Usługi Doradcze, Poland, tax ID (NIP) 8212570137 ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal data described in this policy. You can reach us at privacy@snackbraingames.com.

2. What this policy covers

This policy covers the mobile game Arrowtopia: Arrow Puzzle (package name pl.snackbrain.arrowtopia, "the game"), the website snackbraingames.com and emails you send us.

3. Data saved on your device

The game saves your progress on your device: completed levels, stars and best times, hints, game settings (such as sound and language) and simple counters such as the number of levels played. This data is not sent to us and we cannot see it. It is deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall the game. If you use Android backup, Android may include the game's data in the backup kept in your Google account, according to your device settings.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The game is free and is funded by ads served by Google AdMob, provided by Google Ireland Limited (for users in the EEA and Switzerland) or Google LLC (elsewhere). To show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the game may collect:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and the approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language and app version;
  • ad interactions, such as ads shown and tapped;
  • diagnostic and performance information about the ads.

Your choices. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game shows Google's consent message before any ads. You can allow personalised ads, refuse them or manage your choices in detail. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings, then Ad privacy. If you refuse personalised ads, you may still see non-personalised ads, which use limited data for purposes such as frequency capping, aggregated reporting and fraud prevention. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings, Privacy, Ads).

Rewarded ads (for example, for an extra hint or 5 more seconds) are always optional and are only shown when you choose to watch one.

Legal basis. Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) where the law requires it, in particular for personalised ads and for storing or reading information on your device. Otherwise, our legitimate interest in funding a free game and keeping ads safe (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Google also uses this data for its own purposes as an independent controller. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Purchases and analytics

The game currently has no in-app purchases. It does not use separate analytics or tracking tools: gameplay events are only counted in the device's memory and never leave it. If this changes, we will update this policy before the change goes live.

6. Emails

When you email us, we process your email address, your name if you give it, and the content of your message, so that we can reply and help you. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). We keep emails for as long as needed to handle your matter and then for up to 2 years, unless we need them longer to establish or defend legal claims. Our email provider is Zoho Corporation B.V. (Netherlands), which stores our mailbox in its EU data centres and processes it on our behalf.

7. The website

snackbraingames.com uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party scripts or fonts. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may record technical data such as your IP address to deliver the site and keep it secure. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in running a safe website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

8. Transfers outside the EEA

Google and GitHub may process data outside the European Economic Area, including in the United States. These transfers rely on the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and/or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

9. Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has shared personal data with us, please contact us and we will delete it.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing before the withdrawal. To use these rights, write to privacy@snackbraingames.com. Because we do not keep game data on our servers, the quickest way to delete game data is to clear the app's data or uninstall the game, and to reset your advertising ID. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority. In Poland this is the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (Prezes Urzędu Ochrony Danych Osobowych), ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warszawa, uodo.gov.pl.

11. Security

The game keeps your data on your device and connects to the internet only to load ads. Our mailbox and accounts are protected with strong passwords and two-step verification.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a feature or a game. We will change the date at the top, and tell you in the game or in the store listing about important changes.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@snackbraingames.com.
